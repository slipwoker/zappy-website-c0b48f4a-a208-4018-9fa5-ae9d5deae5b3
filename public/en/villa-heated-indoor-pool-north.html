Between the Mountains of the North and the Sea of Galilee

Villa Beit HaLogs is located in the heart of Moshava Migdal, within touching distance of the shores of the Sea of Galilee and the hiking trails of the Galilee. Here, between the scent of orchards and the crisp lake air, the familiar and beloved landscape of northern Israel unfolds — peaceful, simple, and full of warmth.

The location allows for a perfect combination of complete privacy at the villa and quick access to everything the area has to offer: morning walks to the water, local markets, fish restaurants, and historical sites.

Questions We're Often Asked

Everything You Need to Know Before You Arrive

Answers to the most frequently asked questions, so you can plan your family vacation with complete peace of mind.

Is the Covered Pool Really Heated Year-Round?

Yes! Our covered pool is heated to a pleasant temperature of 29 degrees throughout all seasons of the year. Even on the coldest winter days, you can enjoy swimming and relaxing in warm water, inside a sheltered and indulgent space with soft lighting and an intimate atmosphere.

How Many Families Can Stay at the Villa Comfortably?

The villa was specially designed for shared vacations and is suitable for hosting up to three families simultaneously. With five spacious bedrooms, large dining areas, and pleasant communal spaces, each family enjoys full privacy while also experiencing a true sense of togetherness.

How Far Is It from the Sea of Galilee and What Attractions Are Nearby?

The villa is situated on a hill overlooking the Sea of Galilee landscapes, a short drive from the swimming beaches and promenade. Beyond the stunning views, you can enjoy a variety of attractions: hiking trails, wineries, chef restaurants, and heritage sites. Our team will be happy to recommend the perfect itinerary for the whole family.
