Villa Beit HaLogs was built on the belief that the most beautiful moments happen when families come together. Here you will find generous space, a warm old-world atmosphere, and a breathtaking view of the Sea of Galilee — all in one place.

Frequently Asked Questions About the Villa on the Sea of Galilee

All the answers to the important questions — in one place

How many people is the villa suitable for?

The villa accommodates 4 families, up to 18 guests. There are 5 bedrooms: 3 suites (a master room and a children's space, up to 6 people per suite, with jacuzzi and shower) and 2 additional rooms for 3 to 4 people each.

Is the pool heated? Can you swim in winter?

Yes. The pool is covered and heated and operates year-round, even in winter.

Where is the villa located?

At 47 HaHartzit Street in Moshava Migdal, in the Lower Galilee, with a view of the Sea of Galilee. The Sea of Galilee is a 5-minute drive away, ancient Tiberias is 10 minutes, and the Arbel Cliffs and Zalmon Stream are within walking distance.

How much does a night at the villa cost?

Prices vary by season, midweek or weekend, and holidays. For an exact price for your dates, message us on WhatsApp at 052-828-2481 and we'll get back to you quickly.

What are the check-in and check-out times?

Check-in from 3:00 PM. Check-out by 11:00 AM, and on Saturdays and holidays, check-out is at the end of Shabbat or the end of the holiday.

Can we bring a dog or a pet?

Yes, with prior coordination. Let us know when you book.

Is the kitchen kosher?

The kitchen is not kosher, but is kept meticulously clean.

What is available in the kitchen and shared areas?

A large fully-equipped kitchen, dining area, living room with fireplace, patio with ping pong table, and in the lower yard a table for 20 people, gas and charcoal BBQ, and a jacuzzi with a view of the Sea of Galilee.

Is there parking and WiFi?

Yes, free parking and fast WiFi.

Is it suitable for families with children?

Very much so. The villa was designed for multiple families together: each suite has a separate children's space, and there is a covered pool, yard, and ping pong.

How do you make a reservation?

Fastest via WhatsApp at 052-828-2481. You can also call or use the booking form on the website.
